Building Off Grid Mesh Networks

Using MeshCore and Meshtastic, we're building a community-driven, off-grid, license free, subscription-free, cell service-free LoRa mesh radio infrastructure across the Crystal Coast along the Southern Outer Banks of North Carolina for personal and emergency communications.

Mesh at the Coast

LoRa mesh networks (Meshtastic and MeshCore) provide long-range, low-power communications at the coast that can relay messages between nodes without relying on cell towers or satellites, extending coverage beyond line of sight and improving reliability during emergencies or outages. They are inexpensive to deploy, use license-free radio frequencies, and can operate for days on battery power, making them practical for boaters and coastal communities. Come help us to build the Crystal Coast Mesh!

What is Mesh Networking?

Mesh networks create a web of interconnected devices that can communicate directly with each other, without relying on cell towers or internet infrastructure. Perfect for coastal North Carolina, where natural disasters can disrupt traditional communication channels, mesh networks provide a resilient and decentralized way to stay connected.

No Cell Service Required

Communicate in areas with no cell service, license free. Each node in the mesh extends the network further.

Long Range LoRa

LoRa technology enables communication over miles, even with low power and small antennas. This is ideal for coastal areas where traditional communication infrastructure may be limited or non-existent.

Encrypted & Secure

All communications can be encrypted with strong encryption. Your messages stay private.
