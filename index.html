Building Community Off Grid Mesh Networks

We're building an off-grid LoRa mesh network with MeshCore and Meshtastic across the Crystal Coast and Southern Outer Banks. The community-run network works without cell service, subscriptions, or licenses—ideal for everyday and emergency communication.

Mesh at the Coast

Meshtastic and MeshCore enable long-range, low-power mesh communications along the coast, relaying messages between nodes without dependence on cell towers or satellites. They extend coverage beyond line of sight, improve resilience during emergencies, and run on inexpensive devices, license-free frequencies with multi-day battery life.

What is Mesh Networking?

LoRa mesh radios enable off-grid text and data messaging by relaying packets across nearby devices, without cell towers or internet. Ideal for outdoor use and emergency response, these decentralized networks extend coverage beyond line of sight, run on low-power, license-free frequencies, and can be encrypted for privacy. Learn more about LoRa.

No Cell Service Required

Communicate in areas with no cell service, license free. Each node in the mesh extends the network further.

Long Range LoRa

LoRa technology enables communication over miles, even with low power and small antennas. This is ideal for coastal areas where traditional communication infrastructure may be limited or non-existent.

Encrypted & Secure

All communications can be encrypted with strong encryption. Your messages stay private.

Emergency Response

Which Mesh Platform to Use?

Network Structure

  • Meshtastic: Utilizes a flood routing method where every device can relay messages. This makes it flexible and resilient, ideal for dynamic environments.
  • MeshCore: Employs a structured routing approach with dedicated repeaters. This allows for more efficient message delivery and is better suited for fixed infrastructure.

Communication Needs

  • Meshtastic: Best for casual communication where flexibility is key. It is user-friendly and easy to set up, making it suitable for beginners.
  • MeshCore: Provides more control over message routing and delivery, making it ideal for situations where reliable communication is critical, such as in military or emergency response scenarios.

Summary

Choose Meshtastic for casual, off-grid communication needs where ease of use and flexibility are priorities. Opt for MeshCore when you require a robust, structured network capable of handling complex communication needs in urban or tactical type settings.

Which devices to purchase for Meshtastic or MeshCore?

Both Meshtastic and MeshCore can utilize the same hardware. This means that devices designed for Meshtastic can also be configured to work with MeshCore, but not at the same time, providing flexibility in your choice of equipment.

Any of the following devices will work on either platform. For all-in-one, off-the-shelf: SenseCAP T1000-E, WisMeshTag, Wio Tracker L1, WisMesh Pocket, Muziworks M1, T-Deck Pro. For base installations: SenseCAP Solar Node P1, Wismesh Repeater Mini, Heltec Mesh Solar. If DIY is more your style, RAK, Seeed Studios, Heltec, Lilygo and others offer kits—add antennas, batteries, a small solar panel and an enclosure for mounting on a building or tower.

Hardware

After you purchase your hardware, it is time to flash the firmware. If you are using Meshtastic, go to the flasher website and flash the latest beta firmware for your device. If you are using MeshCore, go to the MeshCore flasher website and flash the latest firmware for your device.
